# User guide

All in One Clinical Tracker stores everything in the browser on this device. Work through
this guide once and you will know your way around.

## First run

1. Open the app. The **Welcome** screen explains where your data is stored.
2. Optionally enter a display name, a role and a first clinical-hours goal.
3. Tick the acknowledgement — this is required — then press **Start tracking**.

You can press **Skip to the app** instead; you can add a target later in Requirements.

## Navigation

Navigation is a horizontal bar across the top of every page (on a phone it collapses into a
**More** menu at the right):

| Item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Totals, targets, deadlines, weekly chart, recent activity |
| Activity Log | Everything you have logged, with search and filters |
| Requirements | Your own targets and their progress |
| CEU Tracker | Continuing-education records and renewal periods |
| Calendar | Month view of logged activities |
| Reports | Report Studio: summaries and CSV export |
| Quick Add | Fast entry from anywhere: clinical, supervision or CEU |
| Settings & Help | Profile, backup/restore, storage, privacy answers |

**Record Quality Check** (in the Quick Add area, the mobile More menu, and Settings) lists
records that look inconsistent so you can fix them.

## Logging an activity

1. Press **Log activity** on the Overview, or **Quick Add → Log clinical activity**.
2. Choose the **activity type** — clinical, supervision or other. The category list adapts:
   clinical shows direct/indirect work, supervision shows individual/group supervision.
3. Enter a **title** (a short label such as “Intake session” — never a client name), the
   **date** (defaults to today), and either a **duration** or a **start and end time**.
   When both times are filled in, the duration is calculated for you and kept in sync.
4. Optionally add a location and notes.
5. **Save activity**. You land on the record page, where you can edit or delete it.

The Activity Log also has a **Quick add** panel for one-line entries: type, category, date,
minutes, title.

## Targets (Requirements)

Targets are yours to define — the app has no built-in licensure rules.

1. Open **Requirements** and press **Add target**.
2. Pick a kind: total clinical hours, direct, indirect, supervision totals, or a specific
   category.
3. Set the target in hours, and optionally a period (start/end dates).
4. Progress bars appear on the Overview’s **Clinical Journey Map**. When you pass a target
   the badge reads **Target reached** — that means *you* reached the number you set; it is
   not a statement about any regulatory requirement.

## CEUs

**CEU Tracker** keeps continuing education separate from clinical hours: credits are summed
on their own and are never added into your clinical-hour totals. Add a renewal period (for
example a two-year window) to see credits accumulate against it with days remaining.

## Reports and exports

**Reports** (Report Studio) builds summaries of your records and can download them as CSV.
Reports read from your local data only and are labelled as such.

## On your phone

Add the app to your home screen (browser menu → *Install app* / *Add to Home Screen*). It
opens without browser chrome and keeps working with no connection. The header shows an
**Offline** badge whenever you are offline; logging and editing continue to work because
nothing is sent anywhere.

## Settings & Help

- **Profile & preferences** — display name, role, and whether supervision counts toward the
  total clinical-hour figure (off by default).
- **Backup & restore** — see [backup-and-restore.md](backup-and-restore.md).
- **Data & storage** — storage availability, approximate usage, browser quota and connection.
- **Erase all data on this device** — deletes everything in this browser. Download a backup
  first if you might need those records.

## Good habits

- Back up regularly (Settings → Download full backup).
- Keep entries non-identifying: no client names, diagnoses or confidential details.
- Run the **Record Quality Check** before you export a report.
